nextnote

A tiny brain-inspired model that learns melodies as you play them, and shows you what it expects to hear next.

Play a short melody, then repeat it. Click the keys, tap them, or use the computer keys A W S E D F T G Y H U J. After a few notes the key outlined in orange is what the model expects next, and the bars below show its probability for every note.

Surprise over time

Each bar is one note you played: tall means the model did not expect it. Repeat a melody and the bars shrink.

Prediction error for the last note

Orange: the model under-predicted this note. Blue: it over-predicted it. Grey: no error.

Improvise lets the model play what it expects, sampled with the chosen temperature (low = safe, high = adventurous). Saved sessions live only in this browser (IndexedDB).

Shortcuts

A W S E D F T G Y H U J play notes
Space start or stop improvising
Backspace reset the model
? toggle this help, Esc close

Everything runs in your browser (Rust compiled to WebAssembly). Nothing is sent anywhere.
