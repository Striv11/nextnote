nextnote

A tiny brain-inspired model that learns melodies as you play them, and shows you what it expects to hear next.

Play a short melody, then repeat it. Click the keys, tap them, or use the computer keys A W S E D F T G Y H U J. After a few notes the key outlined in orange is what the model expects next, and the bars below show its probability for every note.

Everything runs in your browser (Rust compiled to WebAssembly). Nothing is sent anywhere.
